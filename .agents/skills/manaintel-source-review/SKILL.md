name: manaintel-source-review
description: Investigate a ManaIntel episode, disputed recommendation, missing pick, or questionable listening timestamp.
---

Start with the [evaluation inventory](../../../docs/EVALUATION.md) and [runbook](../../../docs/RUNBOOK.md). Match the episode GUID and attempt, then compare original extraction, effective publication and saved review. Use the narrowest retained source window and record whether evidence came from the extraction transcript, independent ASR or direct listening. Matching catalog fields do not independently establish source claims. For a supported limited correction, retain the pick identity and bind it to the reviewed extraction. Keep unresolved claims pending and raw evidence private.
